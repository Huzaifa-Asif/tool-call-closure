# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Accept feedback gracefully, acknowledge mistakes, and avoid harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, and publication of private information.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the security contact in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for conduct they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
